Student siteTeacher guideStandards auditQuestion audit45-day trackerAnswer keys

Only Days 1–5 are student-ready. Days 6–45 below are concrete implementation work, not lessons or assignments. There are no future-lesson buttons in the student site.

Sequence: week 1 systems; week 2 networks/security; week 3 IoT/data foundations; week 4 data/algorithms; week 5 programming; week 6 programming/AI foundations; week 7 AI methods; week 8 AI impacts/technology; week 9 communication, ethics, and showcase. Each day targets one 40-minute period. The unit boundaries cross weeks where practical.

Release gate for each future lesson

  1. Verify the exact standard and required verb against the source. Author all explanations, vocabulary, complete datasets, and worked examples.
  2. Build the actual keyboard-operable activity with feedback, retry, reset where useful, and export. No outside research or account-dependent student task.
  3. Write and independently solve the questions. Add a precise source passage for every item and every activity instruction; review reading level and timing.
  4. Add teacher model answers, performance rubrics, review/test exports, and extend automated interaction/link tests. Manually inspect mobile, keyboard, and print views.
  5. Update the standards audit only after every required part of the expectation has implemented performance evidence. Pilot pacing in class; adjust before broad use.
