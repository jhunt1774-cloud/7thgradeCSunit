Known coverage constraints

The future voice-command standard AI.NI.7.a requires more than a typed chatbot. Browser speech support and school microphone policies must be tested before that lesson can count as implemented. A text-only fallback can teach parsing, but must remain partial coverage of voice commands. Future spreadsheet, code, data, and AI standards require students to create artifacts; recognition questions will not substitute for creation.
