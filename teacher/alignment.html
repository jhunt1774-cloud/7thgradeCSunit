Review method: for each item, locate the taught fact or process, solve it using that passage, check all distractors against supplied facts, and verify that the keyed answer is the only defensible option. For open responses and labs, compare required steps to readings and worked examples; use the teacher rubric rather than word matching. Automated tests validate source references, quoted evidence for knowledge questions, keys, and cross-file consistency. They cannot prove age suitability or the quality of reasoning; teacher review and a classroom pilot remain necessary.
